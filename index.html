Questions

Fair questions, short answers

Will I need new software?

Sometimes. Many fixes are settings in tools you already have, like Jobber or your phone system, and a phone, a calendar and a spreadsheet is a fine place to start. When something new would help, you'll know what it is, why and what it costs before anything starts.

What does it cost?

The audit is free. If you want help with the fixes, you get one fixed price before anything starts. No hourly billing and no long contract.

What happens after setup?

It runs on its own, in your accounts. If you'd like someone to keep an eye on it, or help with your Google Ads, we can quote that separately.

Who owns the accounts?

You do. Everything is set up in your name and billed to you, and we're added as a user. If we stop working together, nothing leaves with us.

Do you only work with trades?

Mostly heating, cooling and plumbing, because the fixes are similar from shop to shop. If you run another kind of local service business with the same problems, ask anyway.
