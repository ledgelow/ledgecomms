Questions

Fair questions, short answers

What does it cost?

The audit is free. Everything after it has a plain, fixed price that you see before anything starts, and running your ads is a monthly fee that follows your ad budget, with no contract. Your ad budget is paid to Google directly. See pricing.

How much should I spend on ads?

Enough for Google to learn what works in your area. For most local service businesses that starts around $750 a month. Your free audit shows roughly how many people search for what you do near you, and you can change the budget any time.

Who owns the account?

You do. The Google Ads account is in your name, with your card on it, and you pay Google directly. We work in it as your manager. If you stop, the account, the ads, and their history stay with you.

What happened to Google Guaranteed?

Google replaced it with a "Google Verified" badge in late 2025, and the money-back guarantee that came with it has ended. The ads themselves are still there: Local Services Ads, the listings at the top of many local searches. You pay for each lead instead of each click, and Google checks your business, licence, and insurance first. Not every kind of business qualifies, and your audit shows whether they appear on your searches.

Is there a contract?

No. Management is month to month. If you want to stop, tell us and it stops at the end of the month.

How will I know it's working?

Calls and forms from your ads are tracked from the start, and you get a short report each month: how many calls came in, what they cost, and what we changed.

Do you only work with trades?

No. Trades are named here because their searches look alike, but any local business people search for on their phones is a good fit. Have something different in mind? We're always open to interesting projects, big or small. Email ledgecommunications@gmail.com and tell us about it.
