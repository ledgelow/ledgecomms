Where leads get missed

Busy shops lose jobs in the same few places

A call goes to voicemail while you're on a job. A web request sits until the next morning. A quote goes out and nobody checks back. None of it is anyone's fault, and most of it can be handled automatically.

  • Calls you can't pick up

    Missed calls get a text back right away, and urgent ones like no heat still reach you.

  • Web requests

    People who fill in your form hear back in minutes instead of the next day.

  • Booking

    Customers can pick a time for a visit without a round of phone tag.

  • Quotes

    Quotes that go quiet get a friendly follow-up, so nobody has to remember to chase them.

  • Reviews

    Customers get asked for a Google review once the job is paid, while it's still fresh.

  • Repeat work

    Past customers hear from you before the busy season, before they call someone else.

What we build

What a typical setup covers

Every business is set up a little differently, but the pieces are usually the same. If you recognize a few of the names below, that's enough to start.

  • Getting found

    Google Business Profile, Google Ads, Local Services Ads, your website

    Leads come in from more than one place. Each one lands somewhere you'll see it, with where it came from.

  • Answering

    Your phone system, missed-call text back, website forms

    Calls you miss get a text back, and urgent ones still ring through to you.

  • Booking

    Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Google Calendar

    Customers book a visit straight into the calendar you already use.

  • Quotes and follow-up

    Jobber quotes, QuickBooks estimates, a simple CRM

    Quotes that go quiet get a follow-up on a schedule, not whenever someone remembers.

  • Reviews and repeat work

    Google reviews, email and text reminders

    Happy customers get asked for a review, and past ones hear from you before the season turns.

  • Reporting

    Google Analytics, call tracking, a one-page weekly summary

    Calls answered, response times, quotes won and where leads came from, on one page.

A good fit if

  • You get leads from Google, referrals or ads, and some of them go quiet.
  • You or your office answer the phone between jobs.
  • You run on Jobber, Housecall Pro or similar, or on a phone, a calendar and a spreadsheet.
  • You'd rather have it set up and handed over than learn another tool.

Questions

What owners usually ask

Will I need new software?

Sometimes. Many fixes are settings in tools you already have, like Jobber or your phone system. When something new would help, you'll know what it is, why, and what it costs before anything starts.

What does it cost?

The audit is free. If you want help with the fixes, you get one fixed price before anything starts. No hourly billing and no long contract.

Do you only work with trades?

Mostly heating, cooling and plumbing, because the fixes are similar from shop to shop. If you run another kind of local service business with the same problems, ask anyway.

I don't use Jobber. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Plenty of businesses run on a phone, a calendar and a spreadsheet. We'll work with what you have.

Who owns the accounts?

You do. Everything is set up in your name and billed to you, and Ledge is added as a user. If we stop working together, nothing leaves with us.

What if I'm not interested after the audit?

Then keep the audit. You'll get a couple of short follow-up emails, and you can unsubscribe from any of them.
