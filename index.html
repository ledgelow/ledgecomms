Questions

Fair questions, short answers

Will I need new software?

Ideally not. We work inside what you already have, whether that's a full setup in Jobber or Housecall Pro, or a phone, a calendar and a spreadsheet. If a new tool is truly needed, we'll explain why, what it costs and your options before anything starts. No software company pays us to recommend them, so the advice fits your setup, not someone's sales target.

What does it cost?

The first audit is free. If your setup is more involved, we can do a deeper dive inside your tools for a small fixed fee, agreed up front. If you want help with the fixes, you get one fixed price before anything starts. No hourly billing and no long contract.

What happens after setup?

It runs on its own, in your accounts. If you want ongoing support and check-ins, or help with Google Ads and bringing in more leads, we can quote that separately.

Who owns the accounts?

You do. Everything stays in your name and is billed to you. We're added as a user while we set things up, and nothing depends on us, so it all keeps working once our access is removed.

Do you only work with trades?

No. Heating, cooling and plumbing shops tend to have the same gaps, which is why they're named here, but any local business that loses leads the same way is a good fit. Have something different in mind? We're always open to interesting projects, big or small. Email ledgecommunications@gmail.com and tell us about it.
