Questions

About the prices

Is my ad budget included?

No. Your ad budget goes to Google directly, on your own card, in an account in your name. The prices on this page are what you pay us.

Why does the monthly fee follow my ad budget?

A bigger budget means more searches, ads, and calls to look after. The table keeps it predictable, so you always know the fee before you change the budget.

What does the Google Ads audit cover?

Which searches you pay for and which ones bring calls, why your ads miss some searches, whether calls and forms are counted, and the pages your ads send people to. You get a written report in plain words and a call to walk through it.

When is the Google Ads and site audit worth it?

When your free audit finds things to fix on your website as well as in your ads, like no tap-to-call button near the top or a slow page on phones. Ad clicks that land on a page like that turn into fewer calls, so it makes sense to look at both together.

How do you work out what searches are worth?

Google's own monthly search estimates for your town, typical rates for how many searchers click an ad and call, and typical local prices for the work. It's a rough guide to the size of the opportunity, not a promise, and every audit shows how it was worked out.

Is there a contract?

You approve a written plan before anything starts, with the price and exactly what's included. After that it's month to month, with no long-term contract: tell us and it stops at the end of the month. The account, the ads, and their history stay with you.
