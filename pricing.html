Questions

About the prices

Is my ad budget included?

No. Your ad budget goes to Google directly, on your own card, in an account in your name. The prices on this page are what you pay us.

Why does the monthly fee follow my ad budget?

A bigger budget means more searches, ads, and calls to look after. The table keeps it predictable, so you always know the fee before you change the budget.

What does the account audit cover?

Which searches you pay for and which ones bring calls, why your ads miss some searches, whether calls and forms are counted, and the pages your ads send people to. You get a written report in plain words and a call to walk through it.

What if I want to stop?

Management is month to month. Tell us and it stops at the end of the month. The account, the ads, and their history stay with you.

Not sure where you'd start?

The free audit shows what your customers see when they search, where you sit, and what we'd do first.
