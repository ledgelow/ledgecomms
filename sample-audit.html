What I'd fix first

  1. Put tap-to-call and online booking at the top of every page

    Someone with no heat at 9 pm wants to get on a schedule right away. Right now they have to wait until morning or call someone else.

    What it takes: A booking button from the scheduling tool you already use, about an afternoon.

  2. Cut the form to four fields and reply within a minute

    Nine fields and a two-day reply window send people to the next shop on the list. An instant reply with booking times keeps them with you.

    What it takes: A form change plus an automatic reply, a couple of hours.

  3. Ask for a Google review after every paid job

    Your reviews are strong, but the newest one is from May. A steady trickle keeps you near the top when people search.

    What it takes: An automatic text after each paid invoice, set up once.
