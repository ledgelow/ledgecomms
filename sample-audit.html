This is a made-up shop to show what an audit looks like. It has a solid reputation, but anyone who finds it after 5 pm or on a weekend has no way to get on the schedule.

Where leads get missed

  1. Lead comes inNeeds workOn a phone, the number is in the footer. There's no tap-to-call button near the top of the page.
  2. First responseNeeds workThe contact form has nine fields and says to expect a reply within two business days.
  3. Visit bookedNeeds workNo online booking. Every job needs a phone call during office hours to get booked.
  4. Quote sentCouldn't checkQuote follow-up happens after a visit, so it can't be seen from outside. We'd look at it together.
  5. Job done and paidLooks good4.8 stars from 42 Google reviews, and the owner replies to most of them. The newest review is from May.
  6. Customer comes backNeeds workThere's no maintenance plan or tune-up offer on the site, so past customers have no reason to come back first.

What we'd fix first

  1. Put tap-to-call and online booking at the top of every page

    Someone with no heat at 9 pm wants to get on a schedule right away. Right now they have to wait until morning or call someone else.

    What it takes: A booking button from the scheduling tool you already use, about an afternoon.

  2. Cut the form to four fields and reply within a minute

    Nine fields and a two-day reply window send people to the next shop on the list. An instant reply with booking times keeps them with you.

    What it takes: A form change plus an automatic reply, a couple of hours.

  3. Ask for a Google review after every paid job

    Your reviews are strong, but the newest one is from May. A steady trickle keeps you near the top when people search.

    What it takes: An automatic text after each paid invoice, set up once.
